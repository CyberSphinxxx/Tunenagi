## Threats / API rights / privacy / accessibility checks

- No credentials, tokens or signing material in the tree: `secret_scan.py --history` exits 0 over
  every commit, including the new files.
- No provider was contacted by the build, the tests or CI (`check_ci.py` C5 rejects provider
  hosts); there is no live Audius/LRCLIB/MusicBrainz call anywhere in this phase.
- Manifest requests no permissions yet, matching ADR-003's "only what the app uses" rule.
- Accessibility: contrast is machine-audited (`TunenagiContrastTest`, including the two failing
  combinations recorded as prohibitions); the shell's tab labels come from string resources;
  icon buttons carry content descriptions. TalkBack focus order and font scaling are **not**
  verified — deferred.
- Media/rights: nothing is downloaded, cached or redistributed in this phase.

## Final decision

**PASS**, with the deviations recorded above and in ADR-008/`DEFERRED_VERIFICATION.md`: the
phase is verified on the machine-checkable evidence this host can produce, and it is explicitly
*not* verified on a real Android runtime. Phase 01 may start; the release gates at phases 08/09
inherit the open deferral and must not be closed while the register is non-empty.
